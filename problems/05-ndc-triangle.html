Core Concept
1. Where is the Camera When There Is No Matrix?
In 01_where-is-triangle.html, the vertex shader outputs vec4<f32>(in.position, 1.0) directly to @builtin(position) without any lookAt or perspective matrix. The GPU divides $(x, y, z)$ by $w = 1.0$ to get Normalized Device Coordinates (NDC):
$$ \text{NDC} = \left(\frac{x}{w},\; \frac{y}{w},\; \frac{z}{w}\right) = (x, y, z), \qquad \begin{cases} -1 \le x \le +1 & (\text{Left} \to \text{Right}) \\ -1 \le y \le +1 & (\text{Bottom} \to \text{Top}) \\ \phantom{-}0 \le z \le +1 & (\text{Near} \to \text{Far}) \end{cases} $$
Your monitor acts as a fixed window on the $z = 0$ XY plane, looking straight into the screen along $+Z$. Only $(x, y)$ determine where a vertex lands on the 2D canvas; $z$ is used only for depth clipping ($0 \le z \le 1$) and depth testing.
Step 1 — 2D Screen Projection & Area
2. Why Lying on the XZ Plane Makes It Disappear
Projecting vertices $V_0, V_1, V_2$ onto the 2D screen drops $z$. The signed 2D area in NDC is computed via the 2D cross product (shoelace determinant):
$$ \Delta = (x_1 - x_0)(y_2 - y_0) - (x_2 - x_0)(y_1 - y_0), \qquad \text{Area}_{2\text{D}} = \frac{1}{2}|\Delta| $$
// Live 2D projected area & winding calculation:
V₀(x, y) = (0.00, 0.50), V₁(x, y) = (-0.50, -0.50), V₂(x, y) = (0.50, -0.50)
Δ = (-0.50)(-1.00) − (0.50)(-1.00) = 1.000
Area_2D = |Δ| / 2 = 0.500  |  (Covers pixels on screen)
📐 When all 3 vertices have y = 0 (on the XZ plane), every $(y_i - y_0) = 0$, so $\text{Area}_{2\text{D}} = 0$ — the triangle collapses into a 0-pixel-tall horizontal line!
Step 2 — Depth Clipping & Culling
3. Depth Range $z \in [0, 1]$ & Winding Order (cullMode)
Even when $\text{Area}_{2\text{D}} > 0$, a triangle can still disappear for two pipeline reasons:
• Depth Clipping: Any fragment with $z < 0$ (behind near plane) or $z > 1$ (beyond far plane) is discarded by WebGPU.
• Back-Face Culling: By default (frontFace: "ccw"), $\Delta > 0$ is counter-clockwise (Front) and $\Delta < 0$ is clockwise (Back). Turning on cullMode: "back" discards clockwise triangles!
