How Triangle Strips Work
The GPU slides a 3-vertex window along the vertex array: the last 2 vertices of the previous triangle act as a hinge edge connecting to the new vertex. To jump to a disconnected face (such as wrapping 4 walls first, then jumping to the top/bottom caps), repeat vertices to form zero-area degenerate triangles that the GPU skips.
