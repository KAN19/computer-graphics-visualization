2D Vector Multiplication: Turn & Stretch

Multiplying two 2D vectors u = [u₁, u₂] and v = [v₁, v₂] means adding their angles (α + β) and multiplying their lengths (|u| · |v|).

Presets Try Turning & Stretching

Vector u = [u₁, u₂]
Vector v = [v₁, v₂]

1. Geometry How Turning & Stretching Work

🔄 1. Turn (Add Angles α + β):
• Angle of u (α) = 14.0°
• Angle of v (β) = 71.6°
→ Product Angle (α + β) = 85.6°
📏 2. Stretch (Multiply Lengths |u| · |v|):
• Length of u (|u| = √(u₁² + u₂²)) = 2.06
• Length of v (|v| = √(v₁² + v₂²)) = 1.58
→ Product Length (|u| × |v|) = 3.26

2. Direct Formula Compute Product Coordinates Directly

Using the magic coordinate formula directly on [u₁, u₂] and [v₁, v₂] (no trigonometry needed):
New X = u₁·v₁ - u₂·v₂ = (2 × 0.5 - 0.5 × 1.5) = 0.25
New Y = u₁·v₂ + u₂·v₁ = (2 × 1.5 + 0.5 × 0.5) = 3.25
→ Product Vector M(0.25, 3.25) ⇒ Canvas Pixel G(318.8, 56.3)
