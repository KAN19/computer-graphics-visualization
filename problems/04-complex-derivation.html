Where Does the Magic Formula Come From?

Step-by-step whiteboard derivation using unit vectors e₁ → 1 and e₂ → i, and why i² = -1.

Step 1 Rename Guiding Arrows e₁ and e₂ to 1 and i

• Red arrow e₁ = [1, 0] (1 step right, 0°) is called 1.
• Black arrow e₂ = [0, 1] (1 step up, 90°) is called i.
• Any 2D vector [u₁, u₂] = u₁·e₁ + u₂·e₂ can now be written as a complex number: u₁ + i·u₂!

Step 2 Geometric Proof: Why is i² = -1?

The arrow i has length 1 and angle 90° (pointing straight up).
When we multiply i × i using our Turn & Stretch rule:
• New Angle: 90° + 90° = 180° (pointing straight left!).
• New Length: 1 × 1 = 1.
→ Length 1 pointing left is [-1, 0], which is -1! Thus i² = -1.

Step 3 Multiply Brackets & Group Real / Imaginary Parts

(0 + 1i) · (0 + 1i)
= u₁v₁ + i·u₁v₂ + i·u₂v₁ + i²·u₂v₂
= (0.00) + i(0.00) + i(0.00) + (-1)(1.00)
Real Part (New X) = u₁v₁ - u₂v₂ = -1.00
Imag Part (New Y) = u₁v₂ + u₂v₁ = 0.00
→ Result: -1.00 + 0.00i ⇒ Point [-1.00, 0.00]
